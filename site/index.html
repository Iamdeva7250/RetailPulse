Sales analytics

Customer segmentation

RFM scoring and clustering of customers.

Churn prediction

Random Forest: accuracy 75.2%, precision 77.4%, recall 79.4%, F1 0.784, ROC AUC 0.813. A customer counts as churned if they make no purchase in the final 90 days.

Demand forecasting

Prophet daily revenue model: MAE £12.9k vs £22.7k baseline, MAPE 29.7%, 90-day horizon.

Inventory optimization

7-day lead time, 95% service level (z = 1.65). 1,310 high-risk products; average risk score 48.6.
